About TRILUME

Students rate each presenter’s ideas with one tool, or with all three: the MAYA Lens (will people accept it? Raymond Loewy’s MAYA principle), the Metaphor Lens (how is the metaphor working? Prof. WonJoon Chung’s Metaphoric Design Evaluation Matrix V2) and the Insight Lens (how much real thinking is in it?). With all three tools, every idea also appears on a 3D map. The Insight Lens map shows the AI obvious zone: where AI-written ideas for the same brief land.

Designed and developed by Aya Al-Shaikhly, School of Industrial Design, Carleton University, 2026.
