About TRILUME

Each idea is seen through three lenses: the MAYA Lens (will people accept it? Raymond Loewy’s MAYA principle), the Metaphor Lens (how is the metaphor working? Prof. WonJoon Chung’s Metaphoric Design Evaluation Matrix V2) and the Insight Lens (how much real thinking is in it?). Each classmate rates with one lens, chosen automatically, so rating stays as quick as one scale pair per idea. The Insight map also shows the AI obvious zone: where AI-written ideas for the same brief land.

Designed and developed by Aya Al-Shaikhly, School of Industrial Design, Carleton University, 2026.
